    A technical practice, tested against our own product first.

    Product 01 · In production

    UniverseIT

    Institutional ERP for schools, colleges and universities.

    A multi-tenant ERP on SAP BTP, built around the society or trust that actually owns an Indian campus. Each subscribing institution is deployed into its own HANA container, and every read is scoped again by campus. It runs the whole institution — admission to alumni, enquiry to statutory return — with a mobile app for staff, students and parents.

    • Trust, campus, department, year, course, section, student — one enforced hierarchy
    • Admissions from enquiry to admitted student, with quotas and document checklists
    • Fees, payroll, donations and club spend posting real double-entry vouchers
    • Examinations, question-grain outcome attainment and accreditation data points
    • Timetable, attendance, promotion and the academic calendar
    • Transport by route and stop, hostel by bed, inventory, library and infirmary
    • Payroll to the statutory file — both tax regimes, PF ECR, ESI, Form 24Q and 16
    • A migration cockpit that dry-runs a legacy load through the real service handlers

    ProficIT

    Your business processes, on SAP.

    ProficIT is one application organised by business process rather than six transactions and a spreadsheet in the middle. It starts from procure to pay, with SAP as the system of record underneath and a Fiori surface a buyer will actually use, and extends across seven processes.

    • Accountant (record to report) — vouchers, bank reconciliation and financial statements
    • Hire to retire — recruitment, offers, leave, attendance, payroll, appraisals, training and expenses
    • Procure to pay — requisition, RFQ and comparison, order, goods receipt, invoice and three-way match, vendor onboarding
    • Order to cash — customer master and a sales desk that posts revenue
    • Plan to produce — production master, work orders and job work
    • Assets, equipment & maintenance — asset register, verification and planned maintenance
    • Quality management — non-conformance, corrective and preventive action, calibration
    • Approvals at every hop — multi-level, delegated, escalated, audited

    Product 03

    DynAppro

    Dynamic approval flow manager — any context, any depth.

    Every application grows its own approval logic, badly, in handler code. DynAppro takes it out: the flow is data, so a new approval point is configured rather than coded. Multi-level and multi-person by design — serial, parallel, or an either-of-these gate — over any object in any application that can call it.

    • An approval point per business object, catalogued and switchable
    • Levels in series, approvers in parallel, and AND / OR gates between them
    • Approver chosen by named person, by role, by reporting line or by threshold
    • Delegation, out-of-office and stand-in, without breaking the trail
    • Escalation and reminders when a step goes quiet
    • Recall by the raiser, and two reject policies — back a step, or back to the start
    • A full audit trail: who was asked, who saw it, what they did and when
    • Embeddable — the flow engine serves any app, not just its own screens
    Ask about DynAppro

    Product 04

    BuilDyn

    No-code apps, from a form or from JSON.

    Describe the thing you want to keep track of — its fields, its rules, who may see it — in a form editor or straight in JSON, and BuilDyn renders the application. The definition is a document you can read, diff and keep in version control, so a no-code app does not become a black box nobody can audit.

    • Entities, fields and relationships defined in a form or authored as JSON
    • List, object and create screens generated from the definition
    • Validation, defaults and value help declared rather than coded
    • Roles and field-level authorisation per app
    • Approvals wired straight to DynAppro when a flow is needed
    • Published without a deployment cycle; versioned, and reversible
    • The definition exports as JSON — readable, diffable, kept in Git

    Partnership

    We are partners to SAP.

    Which is a commercial fact with a technical consequence: we build inside the platform's own extension model, on entitlements we hold ourselves, with a support path that ends at SAP rather than at us shrugging.

    Partnership means we are answerable for the platform, not only for our code. Licences, entitlements and a support route can be arranged through us, and a problem that turns out to be SAP's is raised as SAP's — with the incident, the trace and the reproduction attached, because we can read them.

    It also means we build the way SAP intends the platform to be built. Extensions sit outside the core on released APIs and side-by-side services on BTP. Nothing we deliver should make your next upgrade harder than the one before it, and where a shortcut would, we will say so before we quote it.

    And it is what lets a firm our size run a multi-tenant SaaS: UniverseIT lives on our own BTP subaccounts, with HANA Cloud, XSUAA, the Integration Suite and MTXS underneath — the same entitlements, the same limits and the same upgrade windows your landscape has.

    Engagement

    Four ways to work with us, and we will tell you which one you actually need.

    Most enquiries arrive shaped as one thing and turn out to be another. A "small Fiori app" is usually a data-model question; a "performance problem" is usually a loop that should have been a push-down. We will say which, before the estimate.

    01

    Fixed-scope build

    A defined thing, built and handed over: a RAP application, a set of Fiori apps, a CAP service on BTP, an interface. You get the design written down before the code, the code in your repository, and a hand-over that assumes we might not be here in three years. Priced on the scope, not on the seat.

    02

    Team extension

    Our developers inside your delivery, your standards, your board. Useful when you have the direction and not the hands, or when the ABAP and the BTP halves of a programme need people who have done both. Named people, not a bench.

    03

    Managed support

    We keep what is running, running: incidents, small changes, upgrade regression, monitoring and the annual platform housekeeping nobody schedules. Applies to what we built and to what somebody else built and left. A response time you can hold us to.

    04

    Product licence and rollout

    UniverseIT, ProficIT, DynAppro or BuilDyn licensed and rolled out — configuration, data migration, integration into the systems you keep, training, and support afterwards. The product is ours, so an unmet requirement can become a release rather than a workaround.

    05

    Assessment

    A short, paid look at a specific question — custom-code readiness for S/4, why a report takes nine minutes, whether an extension belongs in the core or beside it — ending in a document you can act on with or without us. No obligation to hire us afterwards.
